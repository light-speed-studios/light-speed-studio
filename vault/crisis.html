Editor's Note

Editor's NoteCrisis on Earth-X was one of my favorite Arrowverse crossovers. But what always stuck with me most were its emotional beats and the relationships between these characters. I wanted to accentuate those moments and give them more room to resonate, while shaping the crossover into one continuous film. More than anything, I wanted the emotion to carry the story just as much as the spectacle.
